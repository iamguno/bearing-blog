Privacy Policy

The Roviatech Blog (blog.roviatech.co.kr) collects only the information needed to answer your inquiry and handles it as follows.

What we collect

What you send us by email: your name, email address, phone number, company, country, the content of your inquiry and any attached photos. We do not collect anything else from you directly.

How we use it

Only to reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For one year after your inquiry is closed, after which we delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which articles are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and does not give us your name or contact details. On the English pages it runs only if you click "Accept" in the cookie notice. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and choose again.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent. Write to the email on our Contact page.

Who is responsible

The blog operator, based in Seoul, South Korea, is responsible for your information. You can reach us through the Contact page.

This policy applies from October 30, 2026.
